## ملاحظات التفسير والفجوات المعلنة

- `MATH-S1-U1A-L04`: Bag labels use exclusive bounds; generated counts never equal a boundary to avoid the inclusive/exclusive ambiguity of «ما بين».
- `MATH-S1-U1A-L07`: Number line drawn left-to-right (0 on the left) exactly as on p.10.
- `MATH-S1-U1B-L15`: Star-making with physical triangles is hands-on; the app practises counting the small triangles that compose the book's shapes (rhombus, trapezoid, big triangle, hexagon, star) and counting sides.
- `MATH-S1-U1B-L16`: Rule from p.19 labels: each separate cube or straight row of joined cubes shows 3 faces from the fixed view.
- `MATH-S1-U1C-L19`: Random packing cannot be computed; the app practises counting neatly arranged layers and comparing them.
- `MATH-S1-U1C-L21`: Making a mobile is a craft; the app practises the lesson vocabulary (balanced, centimetre) with equal-sides and ruler games.
- `MATH-S1-U1C-L27`: Banknote images from p.30 are NOT reproduced; plain value cards are used instead.
- `MATH-S1-U2A-L28`: The book's cards (10, 30, 80, 100, 7, 5, 2) also make 3-digit numbers such as 107 or 137; the app currently builds 2-digit numbers (11-99) and orders numbers up to 107. 3-digit composition is a known gap for review.
- `MATH-S1-U2A-L29`: Comparison sentences are written in the book's RTL form; the open side of < or > always faces the larger number.
- `MATH-S1-U2A-L31`: The book's 30-second challenge is optional in the app; response time never affects scoring (policy).
- `MATH-S1-U2A-L36`: Rows are aligned at the right edge like p.39 (RTL); the child compares the part of the longer row beyond the shorter one.
